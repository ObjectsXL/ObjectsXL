# Security Policy

At ObjectsXL, the security of our platform, the integrity of our spatial datasets, and the protection of our partners' intellectual property are our highest priorities. We take all security vulnerabilities seriously and are committed to resolving them promptly to maintain enterprise-grade compliance.

## Scope

This security policy applies to:
* The ObjectsXL dataset delivery infrastructure and APIs.
* Our automated metadata curation and validation pipelines.
* All open-source schemas, integrations, and tools published in the ObjectsXL GitHub organization.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.** 

To report a security issue, please **[contact us immediately](https://www.objectsxl.com/#inquiry)**. 

When submitting a report, please include as much of the following information as possible:
* A detailed description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
* The specific repository, API endpoint, or infrastructure component affected.
* Step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue.
* Any relevant logs, screenshots, or proof-of-concept code.

## Our Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment:** You will receive an acknowledgment of your report from our security team within 48 hours.
2. **Triage & Investigation:** Our engineering team will verify the vulnerability and determine its severity and impact. We may contact you for further clarification during this phase.
3. **Resolution:** We will work to develop and deploy a patch or mitigation strategy as quickly as possible.
4. **Disclosure:** Once the issue has been resolved and our enterprise clients have been updated (if necessary), we will notify you and may publicly acknowledge your contribution (with your permission).

## Bug Bounty Program

At this time, ObjectsXL does not operate a public bug bounty program. However, we deeply appreciate the efforts of security researchers who responsibly disclose vulnerabilities to help us protect our platform and users.
