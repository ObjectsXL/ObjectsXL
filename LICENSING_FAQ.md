# Licensing & Legal FAQ

At ObjectsXL, our core value proposition is **watertight licensing**. We remove the legal ambiguity typically associated with bulk 3D asset acquisition, providing clear, structured rights for AI training, simulation, and enterprise development.

**What does "watertight licensing" actually mean?**
It means our datasets come with explicitly defined usage rights that protect both the original creator and the end-user. We do not rely on scraped data, vague fair-use arguments, or ambiguous open-source licenses. Every asset provided by ObjectsXL LLC is fully cleared for commercial AI training and enterprise applications under a formal, legally binding agreement.

**As an enterprise, do I own the 3D assets I purchase?**
No. You are purchasing a perpetual, enterprise-wide license to *use* the spatial data (including `.usd`, `.gltf`, `.fbx`, `.blend`, and `.obj` files) for specific compute and development purposes, such as training machine learning models, generating synthetic data, or building digital twins. The underlying intellectual property remains with the original 3D studio.

**Are we indemnified against copyright claims?**
Yes. Because ObjectsXL LLC establishes direct, contractual relationships with our studio partners and meticulously verifies asset provenance, we offer indemnification for enterprise clients using our datasets within the agreed-upon terms. This shields your organization from third-party IP claims related to the training data.

**Can we use the assets in consumer-facing products (e.g., video games or movies)?**
Our standard AI & Spatial Data License is strictly for backend compute, AI training, simulation, and internal enterprise digital twins. If you wish to distribute the raw 3D assets directly in a consumer-facing media product (like a commercial video game or film), a separate Media Distribution License must be negotiated. 

**As a 3D studio, how is my IP protected from unauthorized distribution?**
We strictly prohibit enterprises from reselling, redistributing, or reverse-engineering your raw 3D assets. The datasets are licensed exclusively as raw material for AI perception training, robotic simulation, or internal digital twins. Your original files are never legally cleared for redistribution on other asset marketplaces.

**Do you process custom licensing requests?**
Yes. We understand that advanced robotics, autonomous vehicle simulation, and multi-modal AI projects often have unique legal and compliance requirements. Our legal team can draft custom licensing riders to accommodate specific enterprise architectures.
