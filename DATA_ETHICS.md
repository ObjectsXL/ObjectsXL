# Data Ethics & Curation Framework

At ObjectsXL, we believe the foundation of reliable spatial artificial intelligence is ethically sourced data. As AI models become increasingly capable of generating and understanding 3D environments, the demand for high-quality spatial data must not come at the expense of creators' rights, intellectual property, or individual privacy.

Our **Ethical Curation Framework** is built on four core pillars designed to protect 3D studios while providing enterprise AI developers with absolute legal certainty.

## 1. Zero-Scraping Policy & Strict Provenance
We do not utilize web scraping, unauthorized crawlers, or dataset laundering to build our libraries. Every asset in the ObjectsXL Spatial Data Engine is explicitly opted-in for AI training and simulation use. We maintain a direct chain of title for all data by partnering explicitly with verified 3D studios, rendering houses, and authorized creators.

## 2. Fair Studio Compensation
We view 3D artists and studios as foundational partners in the AI ecosystem, not just data sources. ObjectsXL operates on business models that ensure studios are fairly compensated for the use of their intellectual property in machine-learning pipelines, creating sustainable revenue streams for the 3D industry.

## 3. IP Integrity & Watertight Licensing
Enterprise AI development requires datasets free of copyright ambiguity. Our curation pipeline includes strict intellectual property verification:
* **Asset Verification:** We review studio portfolios and source files to ensure assets are original creations and do not contain uncleared third-party IP (such as trademarked logos or copyrighted characters).
* **Clear Usage Rights:** We translate complex 3D asset licenses into standardized, watertight corporate agreements that fully indemnify our enterprise clients for AI training, synthetic data generation, and digital twin creation.

## 4. Privacy & PII Hardening
Spatial data often captures the real world. For modalities like HDRIs, NeRFs, photogrammetry, and spatial environmental scans, ObjectsXL enforces strict privacy protocols:
* **PII Scrubbing:** All real-world captures are processed to blur or remove Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including human faces, identifiable license plates, and private documents.
* **Location Anonymization:** Where necessary for security or privacy compliance, exact GPS coordinates and identifying geographical metadata are stripped or generalized before enterprise delivery.

## Commitment to Transparency

We are committed to ongoing dialogue with both the AI research community and the 3D graphics industry to evolve our standards as technology advances. 

If you have questions about our curation process, suspect an intellectual property violation, or wish to audit your studio's data inclusion, please [contact us directly](https://www.objectsxl.com/#inquiry).
