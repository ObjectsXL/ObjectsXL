Building advanced spatial AI requires massive amounts of high-quality 3D data, but acquiring it often comes with fragmented file formats, messy metadata, and legally precarious licensing. 

ObjectsXL solves this data pattern separator. We bridge the gap between top-tier 3D studios and enterprise AI developers by delivering a spatial data engine engineered for scale, compliance, and automated pipeline integration.

## 🛡️ The ObjectsXL Framework: Ethical Curation & Watertight Licensing

In the era of AI, data provenance is paramount. We curate dedicated, direct relationships with top-tier 3D studios to provide companies with specialized spatial libraries. 

Our framework guarantees **watertight licensing**. We ensure that the intellectual property of our partner studios is strictly protected, while giving the enterprises purchasing the datasets the absolute legal certainty and compliance required for commercial AI training and enterprise operations.

## 🏗️ Structured, Standardized, and Machine-Ready

Raw 3D assets are not AI-ready. Our platform unifies multiple spatial modalities—including standardized 3D models, NeRFs, HDRIs, and highly-detailed motion capture formats—into a single, robust infrastructure.

* **Format Agnostic, Pipeline Ready:** Our datasets support a wide array of industry-standard 3D formats (including `.usd`, `.gltf`, `.glb`, `.fbx`, `.obj`, `.blend`, and `.duf`) to plug directly into your existing data loaders.
* **Strict Metadata Schemas:** Every asset is enriched with consistent, structured metadata designed from the ground up for seamless, automated pipeline integration and searchability.
* **Quality Assured:** Geometry, textures, and spatial properties are standardized to ensure consistent behavior across rendering engines and simulation environments.
